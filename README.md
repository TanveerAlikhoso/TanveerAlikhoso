## 💬 It works like a chat. Because it *is* one.

> **👤 Visitor:** “Do you fix leaking taps on Sundays?”
>
> **🤖 Pixametry:** “Yes — Sunday callouts in Central London, from 9am. [Source: your services page] Want me to book it?”
>
> **👤 Visitor:** “What about boiler installs in a flat with no gas?”
>
> **🤖 Pixametry:** “I don't know that yet — so I saved it to your **Gap Radar** and took your details for a callback. No dead end. No made-up answer.”

That's the whole niche: **grounded, or it says it doesn't know.**
